Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Souls Coffee. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe experience when using our app and services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to your data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect limited personal information to enhance the user experience, including:

  • Email address and authentication credentials (encrypted)
  • Usage stories and meditation activity to personalize your experience
  • Anonymous statistical data to improve the app and services

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Personalize your meditation journey and suggest content
  • Provide customer support and manage your account
  • Generate anonymized insights to improve our services

4. Data Protection

All credentials are stored in an encrypted format. No one, including Souls Coffee staff or third parties, can access your personal information without your explicit consent.

5. Your Rights

You may delete your account at any time through the app. This will permanently remove all your stored information. Instructions can be found in our Help Page.

6. Subscription Management

You may cancel your monthly or annual plan at any time. Refund requests are processed through Google Play. Please review their policy for more details.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in functionality or legal requirements. We encourage users to review it periodically. The latest version will always be published here with the updated date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out at guilhermewnunes@gmail.com.
