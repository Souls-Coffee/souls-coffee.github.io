Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Illumine My Powers, an app dedicated to Bahá'í prayers and reflective readings. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe experience when using our app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to your data.

2. Offline Access — No Account Required

Illumine My Powers is free to use, and every feature is fully available without creating an account. If you use the app without an account, we do not collect or store any personal information about you — no names, email addresses, phone numbers, or usage tracking. The only data stored in that case is the text of prayers and readings you've opened, kept on your own device so you can read them offline.

3. Information We Collect If You Create an Account

Creating an account is entirely optional, offered only for those who want a more personalized experience (such as syncing your data across devices). If you choose to create one, we collect:

  • Email address and authentication credentials (encrypted)
  • Basic app activity needed to personalize your experience

4. How We Use Your Data

If you have an account, your data is used to:

  • Personalize your experience and keep it in sync across your devices
  • Provide customer support and manage your account

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties.

5. Data Protection

All credentials are stored in an encrypted format. No one, including Illumine My Powers staff or third parties, can access your personal information without your explicit consent.

6. Your Rights

If you have an account, you may delete it at any time through the app. This will permanently remove all your stored information. Instructions can be found in our Help Page.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in functionality or legal requirements. We encourage users to review it periodically. The latest version will always be published here with the updated date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out at guilhermewnunes@gmail.com.
