About Illumine My Powers

Helping people align mental clarity with spiritual strength to live more purposeful, peaceful, and connected lives.

Why We Exist

Illumine My Powers was born from the belief that well-being is not just about managing stress or productivity—it’s about harmonizing your inner world so you can thrive in every area of life. Through Bahá'í prayers and reflective readings, we help you awaken the strength already within you.

What We Offer

We offer a growing library of Bahá'í prayers and reflective readings—all designed to help you slow down, reflect, and realign. Every feature is free to use, with or without an account. Illumine My Powers is crafted for anyone seeking a richer, more conscious life.

Our Vision

We envision a world where people lead with compassion, purpose, and clarity. Where spiritual and mental wellness are integrated parts of everyday life. Illumine My Powers is our humble contribution to that vision.

Join the Movement

We are just getting started. If our vision speaks to your heart, we invite you to download the app, follow our journey, and help us shape the future of spiritual wellness together.
