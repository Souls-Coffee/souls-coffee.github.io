ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT

How to Delete Your Account

If you would like to delete your Souls Coffee account, please follow these steps:

  1. Open the app on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab located at the bottom right of the screen.
  3. Scroll down if necessary and tap on Delete Account.

This action will permanently delete all your stored information and cannot be undone. If you change your mind, you are welcome to create a new account in the future using the same or different credentials.

APP USAGE

How to Listen to Meditations

To access the meditation audio content:

  1. Open the app on your device.
  2. Tap the Meditation tab located at the bottom left of the screen.
  3. Browse the list of available meditation options.
  4. Select a meditation by tapping on it to open the detailed screen.
  5. Press the Play button to begin listening to the audio.

If the meditation audio does not play or you experience any issues, please contact our support team so we can investigate and assist you promptly.

How to Change the Language

To change the language of the app interface:

  1. Open the app on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab located at the bottom right of the screen.
  3. Tap the gear icon (⚙️) in the top right corner to open the Settings page.
  4. In the Language section, select your preferred language from the list.

If the language does not change immediately, try closing and reopening the app to apply the new language setting.

How to Change the Theme

To switch between light and dark mode:

  1. Go to the Profile tab at the bottom right of the app.
  2. Tap the gear icon (⚙️) to access the Settings page.
  3. Scroll to the Theme section and select either Light or Dark mode.

SUBSCRIPTIONS & PAYMENTS

Where Is Billing Handled?

All purchases and subscriptions are handled securely through Google Play. Please ensure your Google account has valid payment methods configured.

TECHNICAL SUPPORT

Troubleshooting App Crashes

If the app crashes during meditation playback or elsewhere, make sure you are using the latest version of the app. Visit the Google Play Store to check for updates. If the problem persists, please contact support.

Does the App Work Offline?

No. Souls Coffee requires an active internet connection to stream meditations and sync settings. Please ensure you have a stable connection when using the app.
