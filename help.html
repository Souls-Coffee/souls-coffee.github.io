ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT

How to Delete Your Account

Creating an account is optional — Illumine My Powers is fully usable without one. If you did create one and want to delete it, follow these steps:

  1. Open the app on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab located at the bottom right of the screen.
  3. Tap the gear icon (⚙️) in the top right corner to open the Settings page.
  4. Scroll down and tap on Delete Account.

This action will permanently delete all your stored information and cannot be undone. If you change your mind, you are welcome to create a new account in the future using the same or different credentials.

APP USAGE

How to Change the Language

To change the language of the app interface:

  1. Open the app on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab located at the bottom right of the screen.
  3. Tap the gear icon (⚙️) in the top right corner to open the Settings page.
  4. In the Language section, select your preferred language from the list.

If the language does not change immediately, try closing and reopening the app to apply the new language setting.

How to Change the Theme

To switch between light and dark mode:

  1. Go to the Profile tab at the bottom right of the app.
  2. Tap the gear icon (⚙️) to access the Settings page.
  3. Scroll to the Theme section and select either Light or Dark mode.

TECHNICAL SUPPORT

Troubleshooting App Crashes

If the app crashes, make sure you are using the latest version of the app. Visit the Google Play Store to check for updates. If the problem persists, please contact support.

Do I Need an Account?

No. Every feature of Illumine My Powers is available without creating an account. Signing up is entirely optional, offered only for those who want a more personalized experience, such as syncing across devices. The app requires an active internet connection to load prayers and readings.
